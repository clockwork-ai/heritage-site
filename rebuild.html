    Before the offer

    Broken, rebuilt,and rebuilt again.

    I've got an addictive personality. For most of my life it worked against me. What changed wasn't willpower. I had plenty of that, and it never once saved me. It was structure.

    Drink took its time. Nothing dramatic, no single day it started. Just more, slightly more often, over years, until I was getting through close to a litre of vodka most nights, smoking twenty to thirty a day, and eating whatever I could grab between working.

    Wake up, work, eat junk, drink until I passed out, repeat.

    There's a photograph of me from November 2018, taken just before midnight. I'm unconscious on my own stairs. There's a stair gate in the shot. I couldn't bear to look at myself in the mirror.

    It was the mornings, mostly. No energy. Just pushing through the day to get home, sit down and drink for comfort. Hanging on all day to feel something I should have been feeling anyway. Happy. Content. With my family. Not waiting on a drink for it.

    I stopped drinking. A week later, I stopped smoking. The two things that were running my life, gone in a fortnight. The hard part was what came after. Put one thing down and something else quietly picks up the slack.

    The full account is on the about page. This is the short version, because you should know who is asking you to trust him with a year.
