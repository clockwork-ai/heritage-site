Your training

A ladder you climb at your own pace.

You start on Foundation and move up as you get stronger. On every rung I load a starter programme into your app, then you add your working sets and swap in exercises from the video library. Ready to step up? Just ask and I'll activate the next rung.

Rung 1 · 3-day

Foundation

Where everyone starts. Full body, three times a week: everything trained often, low fatigue, and the base every other rung is built on.

Rung 2 · 4-day

Upper / Lower

Two upper days, two lower: more volume per muscle and room to push the big lifts. Activated when Foundation starts to feel light.

Rung 3 · 5-day

Push / Pull / Legs +

The top rung. A push, pull and legs base plus an extra upper and lower to drive weak points. Maximum volume, once you're training five days a week.
