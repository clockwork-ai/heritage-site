    Premium 1:1 coaching · terms v3 · October 2026

    The terms,in plain English.

    This is what you're stepping into. Nothing here is buried, and every number stays visible to you the whole way through.

    Applies to every 1:1 package
    01

    What acceptance means

    • Payment secures your place. The payment link comes first, and your onboarding form arrives the moment it's paid.
    • Full premium from day one: coached training and cardio, your nutrition build and the weekly check-in rhythm. The complete programme, not a roadmap.
    • Programmes start on a Monday. The days between payment and your first Monday are your setup window: the app, baseline photos, measurements and your first weigh-in.
    02

    The standard: Structure Driven Results (SDR)

    One weekly score, 0 to 100%, averaged across four pillars. It scores what you control, not the outcome, and everything that's scored is visible to you at all times.

    PillarWhat countsWeight
    NutritionDays on your calorie and macro target, out of 725%
    TrainingSessions done, out of sessions set25%
    StepsDays hitting your step target, out of 725%
    Check-inWeekly review on time and complete: weigh-ins, waist and body measurements, progress photos25%

    If adherence drops: three weeks in a row below 80% triggers a review call. We find which pillar slipped and why, and reset the plan around it.

    03

    The 8-Week Progress Guarantee

    Hold 80%+ on your weekly SDR score for the first eight weeks. If nothing has improved, the next month is on me.

    • Applies to every 1:1 package.
    • The window is your first eight weeks of coaching.
    • The condition is a score of 80% or more at every weekly check-in in that window, using the four pillars in section 2.
    • A result is any one of: a drop on the scale, at the waist or in your body measurements, or better sleep and eating habits, comparing week 8 to week 1. Eight weeks of honest tracking is more than enough time to see one.
    • Monthly plans: if nothing has improved, month three is free of charge.
    • Paid in full: if nothing has improved, a free month is added to the end of your plan. If the score holds 80%+ through that month and still nothing has improved, a second free month is added. Two free months is the maximum.
    • No data, no guarantee. The check-in pillar is what proves the work was done. It's free coaching time, not a refund.
    04

    The money

    • Payment plans are spread monthly across the whole programme, not front-loaded: 3 payments across 12 weeks, 6 across 6 months, 12 across 12 months. The first payment secures your place, and the rest fall on the same date each month.
    • 14-day cooling-off from the day of purchase, less the value of coaching already delivered.
    • Missed instalment: you get a 7-day grace window to settle. After that, coaching pauses until the balance is up to date.
    • Leaving early: you can stop at any time. Nothing further is owed beyond instalments already due, and coaching already delivered isn't refunded.
    05

    Application and fit

    Coaching is application only because the fit has to be right for both of us. I read every application myself. If I don't think I'm the right coach for you, I'll tell you straight rather than take your money.

    06

    Your data

    • Everything that scores you is visible to you: every weigh-in, every log, every percentage, at all times.
    • Your data is used to coach you and nothing else. It's never sold. It's held in Everfit, the coaching app, and in our WhatsApp chat, and isn't shared with anyone else.
    • You can ask for a copy, or for it to be deleted, at any time.
    07

    The medical line

    I'm your coach, not your doctor. Nothing in this process is medical advice. GLP-1 dosing, medication changes and anything health-related stay with your prescriber or GP, always. Tell me what they decide, and I'll build the coaching around it.

    Agreement: by making payment you're accepting the terms above: the standard in section 2, the guarantee in section 3, the money in section 4, and the shared understanding that adherence, not the outcome, is what's being measured.
