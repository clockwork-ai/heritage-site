    01

    Why online, when I'm just down the road

    Most personal training in Stockport is sold by the session. Bark lists local sessions at £40 to £54, an average of £47 (Bark, Personal Trainers in Stockport). Three a week at that average comes to about £600 a month, and it covers three hours of your week.

    The other 165 hours are where results are actually decided. What you eat on a Friday night. Whether you sleep. Whether you move on the days you don't train. Online coaching is built around that part of the week, not the hour in the gym.

    So you train wherever you already train. A chain gym in town, a Life Leisure centre, a garage set-up at home. I see every session in the app, every meal you log and every number you send me, and once a week I decide the one thing to change next.

    02

    What coaching includes

    • Calibrated macros. Your real maintenance first, then protein, carbs and fats set to your goal.
    • A structured programme. Three gym sessions a week and 10,000 steps a day as the baseline, with progression built in.
    • Weekly check-ins. Food, training, steps, sleep and resting heart rate, read together. One adjustment at a time.
    • Video feedback. Film your sets and get timestamped notes back within 48 hours.
    • WhatsApp, answered within 24 hours. A direct line to me, not an assistant.
    • Bloods read with you. TRT-aware coaching. Medication decisions stay with you and your prescriber.

    The full breakdown is on the coaching page.

    03

    Who this is for

    Men with jobs, kids and busy weeks. You've done the plans. You've restarted more than once, and you got to the end of week two again. You don't need more motivation. You need someone reading your numbers every week and telling you what to change.

    It isn't for you if you want a six-week shred and then back to chaos, or if you won't track what you eat. I'd rather tell you that now.

    Questions

    Do I need to live in Stockport?

    No. I'm based in Stockport, but coaching is online, so it works the same wherever you are in the UK. Clients train in their own gym, on their own schedule.

    Can I train at my own gym?

    Yes. Your programme is written for the gym you already use, whether that's a big chain, a leisure centre or kit at home. You log every session in the app and I review it.

    How much does an online personal trainer cost?

    My 1:1 coaching starts at £1,200 for 12 weeks, about £100 a week, with monthly payment plans on every package. The Ninety is £297 for 90 days, and Physique OS is £29 a month.

    I'm a beginner. Is this too advanced?

    Not at all. The system scales from a 3-day full-body foundation upward. Beginners often progress fastest because the structure removes the guesswork from day one.
