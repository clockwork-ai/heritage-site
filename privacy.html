Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how I collect and use your personal data when you use this website or my coaching. I'm Callum Broadfoot, sole trader trading as CALLUM.B, and I'm the data controller.

Email me at hello@callumbcoaching.co.uk. Post: [postal address: Callum to confirm].

What I collect

  • Contact and enquiry details: name, email, phone and anything you write when you submit an application or download a free guide.
  • Coaching data, if you become a client: stats, goals, training and nutrition info, and progress you share with me.
  • Chat: what you type into Ask CALLUM.B, and your name, email and goal if you leave them there.
  • A photo and the figures you type, if you use the body-fat photo step on the TDEE calculator. The photo is not stored.
  • Booking details you enter in the call calendar.
  • WhatsApp: messages you send me there, if you choose to write that way.
  • Payment data: handled by Stripe when you pay or open the customer portal. I don't store your card details.
  • Usage data: basic, privacy-friendly website analytics (pages visited) to improve the site.

How I use it and the legal basis

To respond to your enquiry, deliver guides and coaching, take payment, answer the chat, book a call, and (with your consent) send occasional coaching emails. Legal bases: consent (marketing emails, and the health information described below, which you can withdraw anytime), contract (delivering coaching you've paid for), and legitimate interests (running and improving my business, including the chat and the site).

Who I share it with

I don't sell your data. These providers process it so I can run the site and the coaching. Each one only does the job I use them for.

  • Vercel: hosts the site and runs the apply, guide signup and chat APIs. A copy of chat leads is stored in Vercel Blob.
  • OpenAI, through the Vercel AI Gateway: Ask CALLUM.B sends what you type to OpenAI. The code names the model openai/gpt-4.1-mini unless the hosting setting names a different model. The body-fat photo on the TDEE calculator goes the same way. That photo is not stored.
  • MailerLite: the email list for the free guide, the calculators, applications and chat signups.
  • Formspree: receives the forms, including applications, the guide and the calculators, and can send me an alert from the chat.
  • Cal.com: the booking calendar on the call page. What you enter there goes to Cal.com.
  • Stripe: payment links and the customer portal. You enter card details on Stripe. I don't keep them.
  • Everfit: the coaching app. If you join me for 1:1, the health information below is held there.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): if you message me on WhatsApp, or ask me to reply there, Meta carries that chat.
  • Plausible: page visit counts. The script is on the pages.
  • Google Fonts: the pages load their typefaces from Google, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them.
  • Google Analytics 4 and the Meta pixel: only if you accept cookies. The Cookies section says how that choice works.

Some of these providers, including Stripe, Formspree, MailerLite, Google and Meta, may process your data outside the UK. Others on the list above may do the same. Where they do, the transfer sits under a UK adequacy regulation or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. This is a general note. It does not set out each contract.

Health information and progress photos (1:1 coaching clients)

If you join me for 1:1 coaching, I ask for health information so I can coach you safely. This section covers that extra information. The rest of this policy still applies.

  • What I collect: your answers to the health and medical questionnaire, including the PAR-Q, plus any injuries, your medication, your measurements and weigh-ins, your progress photos and an emergency contact.
  • Why: only to plan and adjust your coaching and to keep you safe.
  • Legal basis: your explicit consent, which you give in the Everfit health check form. You can withdraw it at any time by telling me.
  • Where it's held: in the Everfit coaching app.
  • Photos: I never share or post your progress photos without your written OK.
  • Who sees it: I don't sell this information, and I don't share it with anyone except in an emergency or where the law requires it.
  • Your say: you can ask to see or delete your data at any time.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask me to:

  • Access the personal data I hold about you.
  • Correct it if it is wrong (rectification).
  • Delete it (erasure).
  • Restrict how I use it.
  • Object to my use of it, including where I use it to send you marketing.
  • Take a copy in a portable form (data portability), where the law gives you that right.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where I rely on consent. That includes marketing emails and the health information below. Withdrawing consent does not undo use that was already lawful.
  • Have a say on automated decisions: where a decision about you is made by automation alone and it has a legal or similarly important effect, you can ask for a person to look at it and you can contest it.

To use any of these rights, email me. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

On your first visit you'll see a banner where you can Accept or Decline; declining means no analytics or advertising cookies are set. You can clear your browser cookies any time to be asked again.
