Where are you starting?

Fill in the short form when you can. A few details so you pick the right starting point. This is for your own record. Nobody builds a plan from it.

If you are unsure which programme to start, pick the easier one. You can move up after you finish the block.

Pick your rung

Choose one 8-week programme and run it as written. Finish the block, including the lighter week 8, before you move.

Home or Minimal Equipment 3-Day. If you train at home, start here. Three days a week, with dumbbells, a kettlebell and bodyweight.

If you train in a gym:

Beginner 3-Day Full Body. New to structured training, or coming back after a long break.

Intermediate 4-Day Upper/Lower. You already have solid technique and you have been training consistently for at least 6 to 12 months.

Advanced 5-Day Split. You are experienced, you recover well from five sessions a week, and your barbell lifts are reliable.

Fat Loss 3-Day Strength + Conditioning. If fat loss is the main goal, and your gym has a sled or prowler and an air bike, use this instead of the level programme for this block. It is a goal choice, not a level above or below Intermediate.

How to progress: keep the load the same until you hit the top of the rep range on every work set with good form. Then add a little load and go back to the bottom of the range. Missed a session? Do the next one. Do not cram two hard days together.
