Structure over motivation

    About Callum

    I've got an addictive personality.These days it works for me.

    For most of my life it worked against me. What changed wasn't willpower. I had plenty of that and it never once saved me. It was structure. That's the whole of what I coach, and I built it because I needed it.

    Where it started

    Broken, rebuilt,and rebuilt again.

    There's always been something. Drugs came first, early on, and I managed to shake those. But an addictive personality doesn't pack up and leave because you put one thing down. It goes looking for the next one.

    Mine found drink, and it took its time about it. Nothing dramatic, no single day it started. Just more, slightly more often, over years, until I was getting through close to a litre of vodka most nights, smoking twenty to thirty a day, and eating whatever I could grab between working.

    Wake up, work, eat junk, drink until I passed out, repeat.

    There's a photograph of me from November 2018, taken just before midnight. I'm unconscious on my own stairs. There's a stair gate in the shot. I couldn't bear to look at myself in the mirror.

    It was the mornings, mostly. No energy. Just pushing through the day to get home, sit down and drink for comfort. Hanging on all day to feel something I should have been feeling anyway. Happy. Content. With my family. Not waiting on a drink for it.

    The way out

    I wanted to feellike myself again.

    It wasn't a straight line. It never is. Here's how it actually went.

    November 2018

    Rock bottom

    Unconscious on my own stairs just before midnight. Close to a litre of vodka most nights, twenty to thirty cigarettes a day.

    Then

    Stopped drinking. A week later, stopped smoking.

    The two things that were running my life, gone in a fortnight. The hard part was what came after.

    The plant-based years

    Away from the old life, the wrong way

    It got me out of the old habits, but pushed me towards processed food to hit protein. My health went backwards: weight on, insulin resistance.

    January 2023

    Stage 2 hypertension

    151/105 pulse 114
    Rebuilt again

    Keto, then carnivore, then structure

    A ketogenic phase stabilised things. Over the following years I got my metabolic health back where it should be.

    March 2024

    Same monitor. Same arm. No medication.

    113/69 pulse 75
    Today

    A balanced, structured diet

    My body handles carbohydrate properly. And I coach the same structure that got me here.

    I

    The problem was never the vodka.

    Put one thing down and something else quietly picks up the slack. Willpower doesn't touch it, because willpower runs out and the personality doesn't.

    The only thing that ever worked was building a structure that didn't need me to feel like it. I stopped fighting my personality and gave it a job. Now I build the same thing for other people.

    You'll recognise some of this

    Maybe not the vodka. The wine that stopped being one glass. The work you can't switch off. The fourth restart this year that got to the end of week two.

    What I believe

    Transformation is won in inches.Fight for every one.

    The meal you log when you can't be bothered. The session you don't skip. The early night you choose. Invisible on their own. Add them up over months and they're the whole game. Motivation won't fight for the inches every day. A system will.

    • i

      Structure over motivation

      Motivation comes and goes. A structure means you're not deciding everything on how you feel that day.

    • ii

      Baseline before direction

      First we find what you actually eat to stay the same. Then we know exactly how much to change, before we cut or build.

    • iii

      One variable at a time

      Change one thing. Watch a week. Then decide. The daily number lies, the weekly average doesn't.

    • iv

      Consistency compounds

      Steady beats savage. The ordinary weeks you just show up for are the ones that add up.
