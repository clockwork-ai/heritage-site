Know your numbers in 30 seconds.

Add your body fat % and see your daily calories straight away. The macro split, a fat-loss and a muscle-gain version, and what to do next come with your name and email. Turning the number into real results is where the structure comes in.

Why I need it: your macros are worked out from it. Two men can both weigh 95kg, but if one is carrying a lot more fat, setting protein off his full weight runs it far too high. At 15% body fat or under, I use your full weight. From 25% up, I use your lean mass. In between, it shifts gradually from one to the other, so there's no sudden jump.

Not sure? How to estimate it
  • Smart scale: weigh first thing in the morning, after the toilet and before food or drink. It is a rough read, so treat it as a guide.
  • Tape measure: use the tape method further down this page. Neck and waist, about two minutes.
  • Mirror check (men, rough guide): abs clearly showing, roughly 10 to 14%. Flat stomach with a faint ab outline in good light, roughly 15 to 19%. Soft stomach with no outline, roughly 20 to 25%. Stomach over the belt, 25% and up.
  • Women: add roughly 8 to 10 points to those mirror ranges.
  • Stuck between two? Pick the higher one. Guessing low pushes your numbers up.
