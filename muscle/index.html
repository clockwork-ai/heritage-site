The bit nobody warns you about

Losing weight isn't the same as losing fat.

01 · The loss

Up to 40% can be muscle

Lose weight without a system and research shows roughly a quarter to nearly 40% of it can be muscle, not fat: the strength, shape and metabolism you're working for.

02 · The rebound

Then it comes back as fat

When the weight loss stops, most people regain around two-thirds within a year, and because it returns as fat, you can end up worse off than when you started.

03 · The fix

But it's preventable

Enough protein plus the right training cuts muscle loss to around 15 to 20%. That gap, keeping your muscle instead of shedding it, is the entire point of this system.
