What's inside

The 5 signs to watch for.

1

Losing weight but getting weaker

A significant portion of GLP-1 weight loss can come from muscle, directly hitting your strength, metabolism and long-term results.

2

Hair thinning or shedding

A documented response to rapid weight loss and under-fuelling, compounded by low protein, iron and zinc. Reversible, if the nutrition is fixed.

3

Energy crashes despite eating less

Less food means a smaller margin for error. Eating less and eating smarter are two very different outcomes.

4

Nausea after most meals

GLP-1s slow gastric emptying. High-fat, greasy or large meals are the usual trigger. It's a food-structure problem, not the medication failing.

5

Weight loss has stalled

A plateau on a GLP-1 is almost always a nutrition issue. Without structure, it's easy to under-eat some days and unknowingly compensate on others.
