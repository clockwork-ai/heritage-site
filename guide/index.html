The Warning Signs

Sound familiar? Most people have at least two.

Up to 40% of the weight lost on a GLP-1 can be muscle, not fat. These are the signs it's happening to you.

01

The scale drops but you look… softer

Losing weight isn't the same as losing fat. If the mirror isn't matching the scale, you're likely burning through muscle.

02

You're barely eating protein, and don't feel hungry enough to fix it

Appetite suppression makes protein the first casualty. On a GLP-1 you need more than a normal dieter, not less.

03

Your energy's gone by mid-afternoon

Under-fuelling feels like discipline until it feels like exhaustion. There's a difference between a deficit and running on fumes.

04

Hair shedding, nails weaker, training flat

Classic signs the weight coming off includes tissue you wanted to keep. Fixable, but only if you catch it.

05

No plan for what happens when you come off it

The jab manages appetite, not habits. Without a system underneath it, the weight comes back. The research is blunt about this.
